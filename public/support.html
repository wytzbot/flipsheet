FlipSheet Support

For installation, billing or automation issues, contact SUPPORT_EMAIL and include the action you ran and the exact error message.

Before contacting support

  • Confirm the spreadsheet is accessible to your Google account.
  • Reopen the FlipSheet sidebar and retry once.
  • For a billing issue, include the Flutterwave transaction reference if available.

Never send passwords, card numbers, secret keys or private spreadsheet exports to support.
