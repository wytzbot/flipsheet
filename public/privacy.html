FlipSheet Privacy Policy

FlipSheet is a Google Sheets productivity add-on. Core cleanup and analysis operations run against the spreadsheet selected by the user through Google Apps Script. We do not need to upload spreadsheet cell contents to our database for those core operations.

Information we process

  • Google identity information needed to associate the add-on with a license.
  • Subscription and transaction references supplied by Flutterwave.
  • Optional notification-device tokens when the user explicitly enables web notifications.
  • Automation metadata such as spreadsheet ID, sheet name, selected range and last-run status so scheduled workflows can execute.

Third parties

Google provides the spreadsheet platform and authentication. Flutterwave processes payments. Firebase may provide subscription storage and optional push notifications.

Data retention

Billing and subscription records are retained as needed for accounting, fraud prevention and support. Notification tokens can be removed by disabling notifications or requesting deletion. Spreadsheet cell contents are not retained by the core cleanup engine.
