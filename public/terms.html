FlipSheet Terms

FlipSheet provides spreadsheet cleanup and recurring maintenance tools. Review important data before applying destructive changes and maintain backups of critical spreadsheets.

Subscriptions

Pro subscriptions renew monthly or yearly according to the selected plan until cancelled. Access is granted after payment is verified. Failed renewals can place an account into a past-due state and eventually suspend Pro access.

Acceptable use

Do not use FlipSheet to process data you are not authorized to access, circumvent Google or Flutterwave controls, or abuse automated services.
