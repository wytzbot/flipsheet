Does FlipSheet upload my spreadsheet?

Core spreadsheet transformations execute in Apps Script against the active spreadsheet. The core workflow does not copy cell contents into FlipSheet storage.

How does recurring billing work?

Paid plans use Flutterwave v4 tokenized recurring charges. Pro is activated only after verified payment processing.

Can I cancel?

Yes. You can stop future FlipSheet renewals from the Plan & billing section. Your current paid period remains available until it ends.
